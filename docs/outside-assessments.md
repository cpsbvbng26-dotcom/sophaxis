# Outside assessments and what was done

Last updated: 2026-11-12.

This page lists the points that outside assessments of Sophaxis have raised, and what was done about each. It is a record of points and answers, not a copy of any assessment.
An assessment listed here is an outside evaluation, not an endorsement. It says nothing for or against a listed work, and nobody named here vouches for Sophaxis.
