# Proposal: the advisory board

Adopted as the plan by Claude on 2026-11-12. The [call for an advisory board](../advisory.md) stays as it is, and there is no adviser yet.

An outside assessment of 2026-11-12 named the dependence on one person as the largest weakness of Sophaxis; see [outside-assessments.md](./outside-assessments.md). This proposal answers how many advisers there are, from which fields, how they are chosen, and what each declares.

## How many

- One adviser for each field that Sophaxis lists, five in all while `site.json` names five fields.
- The board begins with the first adviser who agrees. The call stays open until every field has its adviser.
- A field that gains or loses its place in `site.json` gains or loses its seat on the board.

## From which fields

- One seat each for philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science.
- Advisers come from different institutions.
- Where it can, the board includes researchers who work outside Japan, in places where Sophaxis is offered.
- An adviser does not live in, or stay in, a place where Sophaxis is not offered, as the call already says.

## How they are chosen

1. Anyone who meets "Who can join" in the call writes to the operator by email, as the call says. The operator may also invite a researcher.
2. The operator proposes a name in a public issue, with the field, the ORCID iD and the declaration below. Anyone may comment for as long as the Terms of Use give for the announcement of a change to a policy.
3. After that period the operator decides, and gives the reasons in the issue.
4. The adviser is then named on the contact page, with the ORCID iD and the declaration.
5. An appointment runs until the next yearly audit on the day Sophaxis began, and is renewed when both agree.
6. An adviser leaves by saying so. The operator ends an appointment only with the reasons given in a public issue.

## The declaration of interests

Each adviser declares, before being named, what the call lists under "Conflicts of interest". The proposed form:

```
Name:
ORCID iD:
Field:
1. Positions held now at a repository, publisher, journal, preprint server or index:
2. Payments received, in the period the call names, from one of them:
3. Works of my own, or of a close collaborator, listed on Sophaxis:
4. Anything else that a reader might reasonably see as a conflict, or "none":
Date:
```

The declaration is shown beside the name, updated whenever it changes, and kept with its date when it changes.

## What was decided

Decided by Claude on 2026-11-12:

- The proposal is adopted as the plan, with one seat per field. The outside assessment named the dependence on one person as the largest weakness, and a seat per field answers it without a board larger than the fields it covers.
- The rule on different institutions and the period for comment are as above.
- The call on the site stays as it is. It already says who can join and what an adviser declares, and nothing in this plan narrows it. A later change to the call is a change to a page, not to a policy, and goes through a pull request.
