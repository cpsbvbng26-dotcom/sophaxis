# Before you submit: a checklist

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

Japanese: [投稿の前に：確かめること（日本語訳）](./checklist.ja.md). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

This list adds no rule. It gathers what the [Submission Policy](./policies/submission.md) and the [Moderation Policy](./policies/moderation.md) already ask, so that a submission is complete the first time. Where this list and the policies differ, the policies apply.
For anyone who has never made a preprint public, [Your first preprint](./first.md) comes first.

## The work

1. The work is deposited in an open repository, and its version has a DOI.
2. The DOI metadata carries one of the accepted licences: CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0. The PDF carries no notice of rights that contradicts it, such as "all rights reserved".
3. The work has a title, an abstract and references, in the ordinary form of a scholarly paper.
4. The work belongs mainly to philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics or the philosophy of science.

## The authors

5. The submitter is an author, and their ORCID record links their GitHub account, as [From submission to listing](./process.md) shows.
6. Every author agrees to the listing. From 2026-11-21, every other author confirms their own agreement in the issue or by email.
7. No author lives in, or has stayed since the submission in, a place where Sophaxis is not offered, as the Submission Policy lists them.

## The statements

8. The statement on generative models names each tool and what it did, or says that none was used. From 2026-11-21 it also gives the extent of use.
9. From 2026-11-24, the statements on funding, competing interests, and human participants and research ethics are ready.

## Material from others and personal data

10. Each figure, table or long quotation from others has permission, or is used in a way the law allows, such as quotation. The [Copyright Policy](./policies/copyright.md) explains this.
11. The work contains no personal data of third parties. A person who can be identified, for example in an interview or a case, has consented, and the work says so.

## Examples of a statement on generative models

These are examples only. The statement must describe what was actually done.

- "No generative model was used in writing or developing this work."
- "Claude (Anthropic) was used to edit the language of sections 2 and 3. The authors wrote the text and checked every change."
- "ChatGPT (OpenAI) was used for a literature search, and its suggestions were checked against the sources. No text was generated."

## When there is more than one author

Sophaxis records the authors as the DOI metadata lists them, in that order, and records no roles.
A work may say who did what in the work itself. The CRediT taxonomy of contributor roles is one common way to do so.
Sophaxis does not check such a statement.

## Templates

Two templates for a paper are offered, one in Markdown and one in LaTeX. Each has a title, an abstract, the statements above and a list of references.

- [paper.md](./templates/paper.md)
- [paper.tex](./templates/paper.tex)

The templates are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. A work written from them need not mention Sophaxis.
Using a template is not required, and a work written from one is moderated like any other.
