# Your first preprint

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

Japanese: [はじめてのプレプリント（日本語訳）](./first.ja.html). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

This page is for anyone, such as a graduate student, who has not yet made a preprint public. It adds no rule; the [policies](./policy.html) govern.

## What a preprint is

A preprint is a version of a paper that its authors make public themselves, before or apart from publication in a journal.
It is not peer-reviewed. Readers can still read it, cite it and answer it.

## Before you deposit

- Put the paper in its ordinary form: a title, an abstract and references.
- Read the policy on preprints of any journal you may submit to later. Most say whether a preprint may come first, and some ask for a notice on the preprint.
- Choose a licence. Sophaxis lists only work under CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0. A licence given to a deposit cannot be taken back for the copies already made.
- Get an ORCID iD, and write your name on the paper as you will write it from now on.
- If anyone else wrote part of the paper, ask each of them before you deposit it.

Before choosing, read each repository's own pages on these points. Sophaxis does not compare them, and their pages are what count.

- Whether it charges anything, to authors or to readers.
- Whether it screens a deposit before it appears, and how long that takes.
- How a new version is added, and whether each version gets its own DOI.
- Which licences it offers, and where the licence appears in the DOI metadata.

## Depositing

- Upload the PDF, and give the title, every author with their ORCID iD, the abstract, keywords and the licence.
- Check the record once it appears: the names, the licence and the DOI.
- A deposit is permanent. To correct it, add a new version; do not try to remove it.

## After depositing

- Add the work to your ORCID record, as [Making a listed work easy to find](./findable.html) explains.
- To have it listed on Sophaxis, read the [checklist](./checklist.html) and follow [From submission to listing](./process.html).
- From 2026-11-23, Sophaxis lists a work only when six months have passed since its first DOI, and a first submission needs a researcher to vouch for it, as the [Submission Policy](./policies/submission.html) sets out.
