# Accession register

Last updated: 2026-11-14.

Japanese: [受入簿（日本語訳）](./register.ja.md). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

This page is the accession register of Sophaxis. It lists every record listed on Sophaxis, in the order of listing, and every withdrawal as an entry of its own. The day of each entry is the day in Japan time that the record keeps. The register is built from the records, and nothing in it is written by hand.

## Numbers

The accession number of a record is its place in the order of listing: the first record listed is number 1.
Sophaxis ids are given in the same order, so the numbers follow the ids, and a number never changes.
A withdrawn record keeps its number and its entry, and its withdrawal is added on the day it took effect.
A number that does not appear belongs to a record that was removed, because a removed record leaves every list, as the [Takedown Policy](./policies/takedown.md) says.

## What the numbers are not

The accession number and the shelf mark are labels for this page and for the shelf; neither is an identifier.
Cite a record by its Sophaxis id and by the DOI of the deposited work.
The shelf is a metaphor, and Sophaxis is not a library; the [Name, logo and badge](./brand.md) page says more.

## Feed

A new record reaches the feed of new preprints, [feed.xml](https://sophaxis.vercel.app/feed.xml), when it is listed, and a withdrawal updates the record's entry there.
The register has no feed of its own.
