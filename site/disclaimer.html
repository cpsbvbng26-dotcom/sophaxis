Listed work

Listing is moderation, not peer review. Sophaxis does not check proofs, arguments or data, and does not guarantee that any listed work is correct, original, complete or of any quality. The authors bear full responsibility for their work, including its statement on generative models, which Sophaxis does not verify.

Metadata

Titles, authors, abstracts, licences, versions and dates come from the DOI metadata held by DataCite, Crossref and JaLC, and are shown as received; an abstract that the metadata does not have comes from the submission form. An error there can appear here. Errors can be reported through the link on each record page.

The identity check

The automatic check confirms that the person who controls an ORCID record opened the submission issue, and that the ORCID iD belongs to an author of the work. It is not an official identity check.

They are not responsible for Sophaxis, for its records, or for any error that Sophaxis introduces; see the page for repositories and DOI registration agencies.

Who runs Sophaxis, and why

The operator, who is at present also the only moderator, runs Sophaxis as an unpaid volunteer. No one is paid for work on Sophaxis, and no one pays to submit or to read. Sophaxis is served only within the free services of GitHub and Vercel, at the address sophaxis.vercel.app, and has no domain or hosting to pay for. The operator pays personally for Claude Code, the tool with which the site and its code are written (see How Sophaxis was made below). Sophaxis takes no money from anyone to cover it. Serving unpaid does not lessen what the operator owes readers and authors under the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

Sophaxis began because the operator looked for one place that lists open preprints in philosophy and in mathematics side by side, and did not find one. It is kept for the operator's own reading, as the next section says, and for anyone who works on the same questions. This is the operator's reason, not a judgement on other archives. Sophaxis does not replace them, and a work listed here stays in its own repository.

The operator's own use

The operator keeps Sophaxis as a hobby, as an extension of a personal reading shelf: a place to keep works in philosophy and mathematics at hand and to read them. This use does not change what is listed or how.

  • Only an author of a work can submit it. The operator does not list a work by someone else because the operator has read it or wants to keep it; such a work stays on the operator's own shelf, outside Sophaxis.
  • Sophaxis is not run for profit. It charges no one, sells nothing, shows no advertising and earns nothing.
  • Sophaxis does not publish works. Each work is published by its repository under its DOI, and Sophaxis links to it.
  • A PDF copy is the unmodified file the authors deposited, shared only as the work's licence allows, with its authors, its licence and a link to the original; see the Copyright Policy.
  • Sophaxis takes no copyright and no exclusive licence in any work.
  • A rights holder can ask for the removal of a work under the Takedown Policy.

These choices keep Sophaxis within what the authors and the licences allow. They are not legal advice, and they do not claim that no legal question can arise.

GitHub and other services

Sophaxis runs on GitHub: its code, its records and its submissions (GitHub issues) are kept there, and its automatic checks run as GitHub Actions in the public repository. The site is built from that repository and served by Vercel. Sophaxis does not use GitHub or ORCID sign-in and asks no one for access to their accounts; it reads only public data, through the public APIs of DataCite, Crossref, JaLC, OpenAlex, ORCID and the repositories. Using GitHub to submit is subject to GitHub's own terms and privacy statement. GitHub and Vercel each provide their services "as is", without warranty, and limit their liability under their own terms; Sophaxis cannot promise more about their services than they do. The site is served on Vercel's free Hobby plan, on which Vercel may disable or remove a deployment with or without notice. What their terms provide is set out in the Terms of Use, under "GitHub and Vercel".

Links

Sophaxis links to repositories, DOIs and other sites that it does not control. It is not responsible for their content, their availability or their terms.

Availability

The site is provided as it is, and may be unavailable, change or close. If it closes, the repository with every record stays public; see Continuity in the Terms of Use.

Sophaxis depends on outside services:

  • If Vercel stops, the site is not served; the records stay in the repository on GitHub and in the Internet Archive.
  • If GitHub stops, no one can submit, and the automatic checks do not run; the site keeps showing what it last built.
  • If DataCite, Crossref or JaLC cannot be reached, a submission cannot be checked until they can.
  • If a repository removes a deposit, its DOI stops resolving, and the record is withdrawn under the Withdrawal Policy.
