Policy

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server for philosophy and mathematics. It lists preprints that authors have already deposited in an open repository, and it gives each one a stable record page.

What Sophaxis is not

Acceptance is moderation, not peer review. A listing on Sophaxis says that a submission passed the checks below. It does not say that the work is correct, original or important.

Sophaxis does not host files and does not assign DOIs. The file and the DOI stay with the repository where the author deposited the work.

Diamond open access

Sophaxis charges nothing, to authors or to readers. There are no submission fees, no listing fees and no paid tiers. It will not start charging.

Scope

Sophaxis accepts work in philosophy, mathematics, logic and the philosophy of mathematics. Work that belongs mainly to another field is out of scope, even if it uses mathematics.

How to submit

  1. Deposit the preprint in Zenodo or figshare, under an open licence, and obtain its DOI.
  2. Open a submission issue in the Sophaxis repository and fill in the form.
  3. A moderator checks the submission. If it passes, a record page is published.

The submission issue is public. Your GitHub username and everything you write in the form will be visible to anyone.

What moderation checks

  • The work is in scope.
  • The DOI resolves to the work described, and the submitter is one of its authors.
  • The work carries one of the accepted licences: CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0.
  • The work has a title, an abstract and references, in the ordinary form of a scholarly paper.
  • The submission includes a statement on the use of language models (see below).
  • The work is not spam, not abusive and does not expose personal data of third parties.

A moderator who declines a submission gives a short reason in the issue.

Language models

Work written or developed with language models is accepted, on one condition: the use is disclosed.

The disclosure must name the tools used and say what they were used for, for example drafting, translation, proofs, code, literature search or editing. If no language model was used, the statement says so.

A language model cannot be listed as an author. The authors are responsible for every claim, citation and calculation in the work.

If substantial undisclosed use comes to light after listing, the record receives a notice, and the authors are asked to add a disclosure.

Versions, corrections and withdrawal

A new version is a new version in the repository, with its own DOI. Submit it as an update to the existing record; earlier versions stay listed.

Corrections are added to the record as dated notes. They do not replace the work.

Records are not deleted. A withdrawn work keeps its record page with a withdrawal notice, so that citations to it do not break. The only exception is removal required by law, such as copyright infringement or exposure of personal data.

Removal requests

To request removal for legal reasons, open a removal issue. Do not include personal data in the issue.

Privacy

The Sophaxis site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. Submissions are public GitHub issues and are subject to GitHub's terms.
