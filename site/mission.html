Article 1 (Purpose)

Sophaxis is a place that lists preprints in philosophy and mathematics, and in the fields around them (logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science). If the works it lists are likened to a bookshelf, Sophaxis is a bookshelf from which anyone can take a work without paying. The operator runs this bookshelf free of charge. By doing so, the operator aims to widen the opportunities to reach scholarly work, and to have a better influence on society.

Article 2 (What this statement is)

  1. This statement sets out the thinking behind how Sophaxis is run.
  2. This statement is not a term of the Terms of Use or of the other policies. By itself, it creates no rights or obligations, for the operator, or for authors, readers or any other users.
  3. Where this statement and the Terms of Use or another policy differ, the Terms of Use or that policy applies.
  4. A "better influence" is what the operator aims at. The operator neither promises nor guarantees that it will come about.
  5. This statement is not legal advice.

Article 3 (Free of charge)

  1. Sophaxis charges nothing, to authors or to readers. There are no submission fees, no listing fees, no fees to read and no paid tiers. It will not start charging (Policies, "No fees").
  2. It takes no donations, sponsorship or advertising.
  3. Sophaxis has no revenue. It is served within the free services of GitHub and Vercel. The operator pays personally for Claude Code, the tool with which the site and its code are written (Transparency, Disclaimer).
  4. Being free of charge does not mean that the quality of the works, the continuation of Sophaxis or access to its pages is guaranteed. What is not guaranteed is set out under "No warranty" in the Terms of Use.

Article 4 (Acceptance is moderation, not peer review)

  1. A listing shows only that the submission passed the checks of the Moderation Policy.
  2. It does not show that the work is correct, original or important.
  3. Sophaxis is not a journal. It does not assign DOIs. A DOI belongs to the repository where the authors deposited the work.

Article 5 (Editorial independence)

  1. The moderator decides on each submission under the published policies, and on nothing else.
  2. No repository, funder, institution, author or service can direct a decision.
  3. No listing is given in return for money, a favour or a citation.

Article 6 (Transparency)

  1. The reasons for every decision are made public in the issue of the submission.
  2. The commitments of the policies are kept as a list in verification/commitments.txt, in the public repository. A commitment is never dropped silently.
  3. A change to the policies follows "Changes and closure" in the Terms of Use. A change that takes away or narrows a right of authors or readers, or adds a duty, is announced on the top page before it takes effect, as that section sets out.

Article 7 (Respect for rights)

  1. The copyright in a work stays with its authors. Sophaxis asks for no transfer of copyright and no exclusive licence. A work keeps the licence its authors gave it at the repository (Copyright Policy).
  2. A PDF copy is kept only as far as the licence of the work allows. Sophaxis copies by script only from repositories whose own pages allow files to be taken by script. For a work in another repository, Sophaxis may keep the PDF that the authors attached to the submission issue.
  3. A claim that a right is infringed is handled under the Takedown Policy.

Article 8 (Personal data)

  1. Personal data is handled under the Privacy Policy.
  2. Submission issues are public.

Article 9 (Generative models)

  1. Work made with language models or other generative models is accepted when the use is disclosed. A generative model is never made an author.
  2. The operator uses Claude Code (Anthropic) as the tool with which Sophaxis is written. Claude is not an author of Sophaxis. In moderation, language models assist the review. They decide nothing (Moderation Policy, Transparency).

Article 10 (Law, and the places where Sophaxis is not offered)

  1. Sophaxis is run from Japan. The Terms of Use are governed by the laws of Japan. The operator runs Sophaxis so that the operator's own acts do not meet the elements of the laws that the policies name ("Acts outside these rules that may come near a law" in the Terms of Use).
  2. Sophaxis is not offered in the places listed in the table of the Submission Policy. As of 2026-11-13, the table lists 43 places. For each place, the Submission Policy gives the day from which this applies.
  3. From 2026-11-29, Sophaxis is also not offered in a place where the operator judges that offering Sophaxis would not conform to the law of that place. When the operator so judges, the place and the reason are added to the table of the Submission Policy. The Submission Policy sets out this rule.
  4. The number of places where Sophaxis is not offered may grow, as laws change or as the operator judges. Each change to the table is announced on the top page before it takes effect, as the Terms of Use set out.
  5. The purpose of this statement does not change these rules.

Article 11 (Who runs Sophaxis)

  1. Sophaxis is run by one individual in Japan. It is not run by a company, a university, a publisher or any other legal entity or organisation (Operator and contact).
  2. Sophaxis is not a project of any university or other institution. It has no affiliation with any society, institution or sponsor (Transparency). No institution sponsors or endorses it.
  3. That Sophaxis appears in an index or a directory does not mean that it is affiliated with the body that runs the index or the directory, and it is not an endorsement by that body.

Article 12 (Continuity and closure)

  1. Sophaxis is run by one person, and is planned so that it can stop without loss. Its continuation is not guaranteed. The site may be unavailable, change or close (Disclaimer).
  2. If Sophaxis closes, what happens is set out under "Changes and closure" and "Continuity" in the Terms of Use.

Article 13 (Disputes)

  1. At present, the operator has not engaged a lawyer or any other professional. This statement, the Terms of Use and the other policies have not been reviewed by a lawyer.
  2. If a dispute arises, the operator will respond after consulting a lawyer or another professional where needed.
  3. The response follows the laws of Japan, including their rules on jurisdiction and governing law in international cases, and what the Terms of Use set out under "Law", "Disputes" and "People outside Japan".
  4. This article is not a warning to anyone.

Article 14 (Changes to this statement)

  1. The operator may change this statement.
  2. A change is shown by the date of the last update on this page and in the record of changes (CHANGELOG.md).
  3. A change to this statement changes no commitment in the Terms of Use or the other policies.

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review, and the quality of listed work is not guaranteed.
