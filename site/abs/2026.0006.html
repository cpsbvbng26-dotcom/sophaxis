The Trinity-Infinity Framework: A Conceptual Model with Illustrative Applications in Game Theory, Mathematical Logic, and Mechanical Engineering

Takuya Nemoto ORCID 0009-0000-1406-0547

Abstract

This paper introduces the Trinity-Infinity (III∞) framework, a conceptual model where three finite elements (Trinity) interact recursively to generate infinite dynamics (Infinity). The framework is applied to game theory, mathematical logic, and mechanical engineering, demonstrating how finite structures under recursion yield unbounded systemic behaviors.

Notes and corrections

  • 2026-11-02 The author's errata for this series (https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/trinity-infinity/blob/main/ERRATA.md) record that the verification script that this paper says is distributed with the PDF does not exist (E3).
