Name

The name is written Sophaxis, in Latin letters, with a capital S, in every language. In Japanese it is read ソファクシス. It joins the Greek sophia (σοφία), wisdom, and the Latin axis; the History and name page says more. A record is cited by its Sophaxis id, such as 2026.0001, and by the DOI of the deposited work. On 2026-11-11 (Japan time), the operator searched J-PlatPat, the database of the Japan Patent Office, and the WIPO Global Brand Database for the name Sophaxis, and found no trade mark of that name. A search shows only what those databases held that day, and it is not legal advice. The operator has not applied to register the name.

Logo

The logo is drawn with rectangles and polygons only, so that it looks the same on every device and uses no typeface.

  • favicon.svg: the logo as a vector image.
  • og.png: the logo as a square PNG.

Use the logo unchanged, to refer to Sophaxis or to link to it. Do not use it in a way that suggests that Sophaxis reviewed, recommends or is connected with something it is not.

Field codes and colours

Each field has a code of three capital letters, a glyph and a pair of colours, one for the light display and one for the dark. The codes are Sophaxis's own. They are not taken from any existing classification scheme, and they say nothing of the quality of a work. The ratio is the lowest contrast of the colour against the background and against a card, as the build computes it; the check computes it again and fails if it is below 4.5 or differs from this table.

Shelf marks

A record page shows a shelf mark: the code of the record's main field and its Sophaxis id, such as PHI · 2026.0003. A shelf mark is a label, not an identifier. Cite a record by its Sophaxis id and the DOI of the deposited work. The accession register numbers the records in the order of listing; that number is a label too.

The shelf

The Statement of Purpose likens Sophaxis to a shelf. The top page and each field page draw the listing as a shelf: each record is a spine in the colour of its field, with its shelf mark, and a withdrawn record stays on the shelf, marked. The shelf is a metaphor. Sophaxis is not a library, and it does not keep the works: they stay at their repositories.

Construction and fonts

The construction of the logo shows its grid and measurements, the field glyphs and the band, drawn from the same numbers as the logo. The site loads no font: it uses the reader's own serif, sans-serif and monospaced fonts.

Badge for a listed work

An author of a listed work can show that it is listed, with the badge badge.svg. Each record page gives the badge with a link to that record, ready to paste.

  • Use it only for a work that Sophaxis lists, and link it to the record page.
  • Remove it if the work is withdrawn or its record is removed.
  • The badge says that the work passed moderation. It does not say that the work was peer-reviewed, and it says nothing of its quality.
