The name

The name Sophaxis joins the Greek sophia (σοφία), wisdom, and the Latin axis. It is written in Latin letters with a capital S in every language, and read ソファクシス in Japanese, as Name, logo and badge says.

How it began

On 2026-10-30, the operator laid down the terms and the settings and generated the site for the first time, with no record listed. That day is counted as the day Sophaxis began. It began because the operator looked for one place that lists open preprints in philosophy and in mathematics side by side, and did not find one, as the Disclaimer says. This is the operator's reason, not a judgement on other archives.
