Decisions by year

Each year, the decisions of the year before are counted from the public GitHub issues and the records by tools/report.js, and recorded here. A declined submission counts once under each reason that the moderator named in the issue. Removal requests sent by email are not public and are not counted. Days are counted in Japan time.
