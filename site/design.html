Legal handling of the design

Last updated: 2026-11-23.

Japanese: デザインに関する法的な扱い（日本語訳）. It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

This page sets out the parts of the look of Sophaxis: its logo and icons, the badge, the share cards, the drawings in the pages, the colours, the fonts and the layout. For each part it says who made it, where it comes from, under what licence it is offered, and which articles of Japanese law it touches. It describes Sophaxis's own understanding. It is not legal advice, and no lawyer has reviewed it. The owner asked for it on 2026-11-22, and Claude wrote it. The articles of Japanese law that it cites are quoted in full, as the e-Gov law API gives them, at the end of each part that cites them. The English sentences about an article are a summary, not a translation; the Japanese text is the law.

Fonts

No font file is served, and no style uses @font-face. The style names fonts in three lists, --serif, --sans and --mono in design.js. Each list ends with a generic family, so that the reader's device chooses a font it has. A font named in a list is not sent to the reader. The badge names fonts in the same way.

The share cards are pictures, so their letters are drawn when a card is made. tools/cards.js names DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans first. On 2026-11-22, Claude drew the 5 cards again with tools/cards.js in its own environment, and each came out byte for byte the same as the card served. On 2026-11-23 the card of 2026.0001 was taken off with its record. There the browser drew the letters with DejaVu Serif, its bold, and DejaVu Sans, from the package fonts-dejavu-core 2.37-8. The copyright file of that package names the licence of the fonts bitstream-vera, and says: "DejaVu changes are in public domain." Its condition is: "The above copyright and trademark notices and this permission notice shall be included in all copies of one or more of the Font Software typefaces." A card holds pictures of letters, not a copy of a font file. The site serves no font file.

Copyright

Article 2(1)(i) of the Copyright Act defines a work as a creative expression of thoughts or sentiments that falls within the literary, scientific, artistic or musical domain. Article 10(1)(iv) of the Copyright Act names paintings, engravings, sculptures and other artistic works among the examples of works. This page draws no conclusion on whether the logo, the icons, the badge, the cards, the drawings or the layout is a work. Articles 19 and 20 of the Copyright Act give the author of a work the right to have the name shown or not shown, and the right to keep the work and its title from changes against the author's will. Every page names the operator in its footer. The site uses no picture, drawing or font file made by anyone else.

Trade marks, designs and unfair competition

The operator has not applied to register the name or the logo as a trade mark. The Disclaimer, under "Names and trademarks", records this and the searches of the name. No application to register a design is recorded in the repository. Article 25 of the Trademark Act gives the owner of a registered trade mark the exclusive right to use it on the goods or services it is registered for. Article 2(1) of the Design Act defines a design as the shape, pattern or colour of an article, the shape of a building, or an image used to operate a device or shown as the result of its function, that is aesthetically pleasing through the eye. Article 23 of the Design Act gives the owner of a registered design the exclusive right to work it, and designs similar to it, as a business. Article 2(1)(i) and 2(1)(ii) of the Unfair Competition Prevention Act name, as unfair competition, using an indication that is the same as or similar to another's well-known indication so as to cause confusion, and using another's famous indication as one's own. The logo is made from rectangles only, and the field glyphs from rectangles, polygons and circles. The site serves no logo, mark or picture of anyone else. No search of registered trade marks, registered designs or the indications of others for the logo, the icons or the drawings is recorded. The Disclaimer says that the logo was not searched. This page draws no conclusion on whether any of these articles applies to Sophaxis.

Accessibility

This page calls 障害を理由とする差別の解消の推進に関する法律 the Disability Discrimination Act. The English name is a label of this page, not an official title. Article 2 of the Disability Discrimination Act defines, in its item 7, a business operator as a person who carries on commerce or other business, apart from the state and public bodies. Article 8(1) of the Disability Discrimination Act forbids a business operator to infringe the rights of persons with disabilities by unfair discriminatory treatment on the basis of disability. Article 8(2) asks a business operator, when a person with a disability asks for a social barrier to be removed, to make reasonable accommodation, unless the burden is excessive. Whether the operator of Sophaxis is a business operator under Article 2 of the Disability Discrimination Act is not settled by the text, and this page draws no conclusion on it.

What the checks of the site confirm on every change is set out on the page Accessibility. For the look of the site, they confirm these:

  • every text colour has a contrast of at least 4.5:1 in the light and the dark scheme, and at least 7:1 when the system asks for more contrast;
  • the field colours have a contrast of at least 4.5:1, and each field has a glyph and a code as well as a colour;
  • no page moves: the style has no animation and no transition;
  • every image that carries meaning has an alt text, and every drawing that only decorates is hidden from screen readers and has a title;
  • every page can be used with the keyboard alone, shows a focus ring, and does not scroll sideways at widths down to 360 pixels, at a zoom of 200 percent, or with the text alone doubled.
