Advisory board: a call

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

Japanese: 諮問委員の募集（日本語訳）. It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

Why

Sophaxis has no editorial board and no advisory body; the transparency self-assessment marks this as not met. Since 2026-11-10 the operator has invited researchers to form an advisory board. Until someone agrees and is named on the Operator and contact page, there is no board.

What an adviser does

  • Reads a proposed change to the policies before it is announced, and says what they think, in its public issue or by email.
  • Advises on the scope of the fields and on hard cases, when the operator asks.
  • Gives no decision on any submission. Moderators are invited separately, as the Moderation Policy sets out.
  • Speaks for themselves only. Advising Sophaxis says nothing about the quality of any listed work, and does not involve the adviser's institution.

The operator decides, and stays responsible for the site and its records.

Who can join

  • A researcher in philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics or the philosophy of science.
  • Whose public ORCID record lists a journal article, a book chapter or a book with a DOI.
  • Who does not live in, or stay in, a place where Sophaxis is not offered, as the Submission Policy lists them.

An adviser is not paid and pays nothing. An adviser can leave at any time by saying so; their name then leaves the contact page, and the date they left is kept.

How to join

Write to the operator by email, at the address on the operator's ORCID record, with your ORCID iD and a sentence on why you would like to advise. The operator decides whom to invite, and answers every message. Each adviser is named, with an ORCID iD, on the contact page, only after they agree to it.

Conflicts of interest

An adviser who submits a work goes through the same moderation as anyone else, with no priority. An adviser does not advise on a case that concerns their own work, or the work of a close collaborator.

Before being named, an adviser declares the following, and the contact page shows the declaration beside the name:

  • Any position held now at a repository, publisher, journal, preprint server or index.
  • Any payment received in the last three years from one of them.
  • Any listed work of their own, or of a close collaborator, on Sophaxis.
  • Anything else that a reader might reasonably see as a conflict, or that there is none.

The declaration is updated whenever it changes. An adviser who declares nothing is not named.

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review, and the quality of listed work is not guaranteed.
