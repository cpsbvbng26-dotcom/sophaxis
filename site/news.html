News

What changed at Sophaxis, newest first. New records are in the feed of new preprints; this page is also a feed: news.xml. Every change is in the changelog.

  1. 2026-11-12

    Fifteen more places where Sophaxis is not offered, from 2026-11-26

    From 2026-11-26, Sophaxis is not offered in 15 more places: Argentina, Colombia, Ghana, Korea, Mexico, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Uruguay, Zimbabwe, Kenya, Tanzania, South Africa, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar. Their laws, read at their sources on 2026-11-12, ask for duties that Sophaxis cannot meet, or could not be read at all. The Submission Policy names the law of each. More

  2. 2026-11-11

    A feed of the changes to the policies, and the other language at the top of each page

    Changes to the policies now have an Atom feed of their own, apart from the news. The Moderation Policy says how the criteria for listing are reviewed and changed, and the Privacy Policy that no page shows a count of views or downloads. A page in English or Japanese links its other version at the end of its header. More

  3. 2026-11-11

    The agreed court, a box for each point of the submission form, and links to sections

    From 2026-11-25, the Terms of Use name the Utsunomiya District Court or the Utsunomiya Summary Court as the court of first instance agreed in a form; talks and mediation still come first, and the agreement stays non-exclusive. The submission form now asks a box for each point the submitter confirms or agrees to, and the automatic check refuses a submission until every required box is ticked. Every policy and prose page, in both languages, lists its sections near its top. More

  4. 2026-11-11

    The submission form in Japanese, and three new pages

    The submission form is also in Japanese, with the same fields, and the automatic check reads it the same way. New pages, in both languages, guide a first preprint, tell libraries how to harvest the records, and give the policies in brief, which bind no one. More

  5. 2026-11-10

    A checklist, paper templates, a history page and a page in plain words

    A checklist gathers what a submission needs, with examples of a statement on generative models and two paper templates under CC0 1.0. A page tells how Sophaxis began on 2026-10-30 and where its name comes from, another fits Sophaxis on one sheet, and another says how to submit in plain words, in English and in plain Japanese. Record pages show a trail of links to where they sit on the site and can carry an abstract in a second language, each author page offers one BibTeX file for ORCID, and the site follows a request for more contrast. More

  6. 2026-11-10

    How Sophaxis differs, a glossary, and a feed for each field

    A page sets Sophaxis beside arXiv, PhilArchive, SSRN and Zenodo, as a layer on top of the repositories; a glossary defines the words the site uses; the press page says how to cite Sophaxis itself; and each field now has its own Atom feed. More

  7. 2026-11-10

    A call to researchers, and an accessibility statement

    A page invites researchers in philosophy and mathematics to submit, with what a listed work gets and what a submission needs, and another helps authors make a listed work easy to find. The accessibility statement says what is checked on every change and what is known not to work. Records can now also be read by the month they were listed. More

  8. 2026-11-10

    Sophaxis on X

    Sophaxis has an official account on X, @SophaxisX, for new records and news. The handle ends in X because @Sophaxis was already taken; that account is not Sophaxis. More

  9. 2026-11-10

    A call for an advisory board

    Sophaxis has no editorial board or advisory body yet. The operator invites researchers in its fields to form an unpaid advisory board, which advises on the policies and decides no submission. More

  10. 2026-11-10

    Name, logo and a badge for listed works

    A page sets out the name, the logo and the colours, and a badge that authors of listed works can show. A page for anyone introducing Sophaxis gives a sentence, a paragraph and the facts. More

  11. 2026-11-10

    Statements on funding, competing interests and research ethics

    From 2026-11-24, the submission form asks for statements on funding, on competing interests, and on human participants and research ethics. The Moderation Policy now defines plagiarism and sets out how a concern of misconduct is handled. More

  12. 2026-11-09

    Listed in the Open Access Directory

    Sophaxis is listed under Philosophy and under Mathematics on the page of disciplinary repositories of the Open Access Directory, hosted at Simmons University. More

  13. 2026-11-09

    The site in Japanese

    The top page and every prose page are now also in Japanese. The records stay in English, as their titles and abstracts come from the DOI metadata. More

  14. 2026-11-07

    Version 1.0.0 archived at Zenodo

    The first version of Sophaxis, its code and its records, is archived at Zenodo with a DOI. More

  15. 2026-11-06

    Listed in ROAR

    Sophaxis is listed in ROAR, the Registry of Open Access Repositories at the University of Southampton. More

  16. 2026-10-30

    Sophaxis opens

    Sophaxis, an overlay preprint server for philosophy and mathematics, opens at its address on Vercel. More

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review, and the quality of listed work is not guaranteed.
