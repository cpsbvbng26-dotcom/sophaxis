Submit a preprint

New to Sophaxis? From submission to listing follows one submission step by step, through the automatic check and moderation.

  1. Deposit your preprint in Zenodo, Figshare, OSF Preprints, arXiv, Preprints.org, SSRN or Research Square under CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0, and note its DOI.
  2. Add https://github.com/<your username> under “Websites & social links” in your ORCID record. This is how Sophaxis confirms that you are an author.
  3. Open a submission issue and give the DOI, your ORCID iD, the category and the statement on generative models. You need a GitHub account. The title, the authors, the abstract and the licence are taken from the DOI metadata.
  4. An automatic check posts its result in the issue. A moderator checks the submission under the Moderation Policy. If it passes, a record page is published here.

In the form, you confirm that every author agrees to the listing and that the authors may share this version under its licence. See the Submission Policy and the Copyright Policy.

The submission issue is public. Your GitHub username and everything in the form will be visible to anyone. Do not include personal data you do not want published.

To update a listed preprint with a new version, open a submission issue and give the existing Sophaxis id. To request removal for legal reasons, follow the Takedown Policy and open a removal issue.

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review, and the quality of listed work is not guaranteed.
