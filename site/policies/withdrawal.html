Withdrawal Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31.

Who can withdraw

Any one author of a listed work can withdraw it. A work is listed only while every author agrees, so the listing ends when one author withdraws that agreement.

The request comes from the author's own GitHub account, as at submission. The author's ORCID record must link to that account, and the ORCID iD must belong to an author of the work. An automatic check confirms both and posts the result in the issue.

How to ask

Open a withdrawal issue and give the Sophaxis id, your ORCID iD and a short reason. The reason is shown on the record page, so it must not contain personal data. The issue is public.

A request that cannot be made in public goes by email to the address on the operator's ORCID record.

What happens

The operator aims to act within 7 days of a verified request. The record page keeps its address, its title, its authors, its abstract and its versions, so that citations do not break. A notice at the top gives the date of withdrawal and the reason. The record stays in the lists, the search, the feed and papers.json, marked as withdrawn. The PDF copies are taken off the site, and the record links to the repository only. A withdrawn record takes no new versions.

The work in its repository

Withdrawal on Sophaxis does not touch the deposit. Sophaxis cannot withdraw or delete a work at Zenodo, figshare or any other repository; a request about the deposit goes to that repository. If the repository withdraws or deletes the deposit, the weekly check finds that the DOI no longer resolves, and the operator withdraws the record with a note that says so.

Withdrawal and removal

Withdrawal is the authors' decision, and the record stays. Removal is for legal reasons, such as copyright infringement or exposure of personal data, and takes the title, the authors and the abstract off the site. It follows the Takedown Policy.

All policies

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review, and the quality of listed work is not guaranteed.
