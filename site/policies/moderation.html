Moderation Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30.

Moderation is not peer review

Acceptance is moderation, not peer review. Moderation decides whether a submission may be listed. It does not judge whether the work is correct, original or important.

What moderation checks

  • The automatic check has verified the submitter and the licence.
  • The work is in scope.
  • The DOI resolves to the work described, and the submitter is one of its authors.
  • The work carries one of the accepted licences: CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0.
  • The work has a title, an abstract and references, in the ordinary form of a scholarly paper.
  • The submission includes a statement on the use of language models and other generative models.
  • The submitter has confirmed that every author agrees and that the version may be shared.
  • The work is not spam, not abusive and does not expose personal data of third parties.

What moderation does not check

It does not check proofs, arguments or data. It does not run plagiarism checks. It does not compare the work with the literature.

Decisions

A moderator accepts or declines a submission in its issue. A moderator who declines a submission gives a short reason in the issue. The operator aims to respond to each submission within 14 days.

The submitter can answer in the issue. The operator then looks at the submission once more, and that decision is final.

Conflicts of interest

Submissions by the operator are moderated under the same rules and are marked on their record pages. The automatic check applies to them without exception.

After listing

Corrections are added to the record as dated notes. They do not replace the work. A record can receive a notice when a problem comes to light, such as undisclosed use of generative models.

Each week, an automatic check confirms that every listed DOI still resolves and still carries the licence in the record. A problem is reported in a public issue, and the operator looks into it.

All policies

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review, and the quality of listed work is not guaranteed.
