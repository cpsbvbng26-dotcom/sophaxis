Who these terms apply to

These terms apply to everyone who reads Sophaxis or submits to it. Authors agree to them in the submission form, in their version of the day. Reading Sophaxis is not taken as agreement to anything; for readers, these terms describe how Sophaxis works, and the law decides what each side owes. The other policies are part of these terms. How content is moderated is set out in the Moderation Policy and the Takedown Policy, and every automatic step that takes part in it is listed on the page What runs by itself.

What Sophaxis provides

Sophaxis lists preprints in philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science. It gives each listed work a record page, a search, a feed and, where the repository allows, a copy of the PDF. It does this free of charge.

The authors bear full responsibility

The authors bear full responsibility for their work and for its listing on Sophaxis. This covers:

  • the content of the work, including every claim, proof, citation, calculation and figure;
  • the right to share the version under its licence, and the permission of any publisher;
  • material from others in the work, and any infringement of copyright or other rights;
  • personal data of third parties in the work;
  • the statement on generative models, and any use that it leaves out;
  • the truth of everything the submitter confirms in the Submission Policy.

Moderation does not move any of this to the operator. A listing says only that a submission passed the checks in the Moderation Policy. If a third party makes a claim against Sophaxis or its operator because something the submitter confirmed at submission is untrue, the authors are responsible for it, as the law provides. Authors agree that the record page (its title, authors, abstract and PDF copy) may be taken offline while a removal request is reviewed under the Takedown Policy, and that OAI-PMH then reports the record as deleted until it is put back. Text written in the forms is shared as the Licence Policy sets out. The operator still acts on requests under the Takedown Policy. A co-author who has not agreed to these terms in a form is bound by them only as far as the law makes them so; for them, this section describes what the law already places on the authors of a work.

What readers may do

Readers may read, download and reuse each listed work under its own licence. The text of this site may be reused under CC BY 4.0, as set out in the Licence Policy.

What is not allowed

  • Submitting work of which you are not an author.
  • Submitting material that infringes copyright or other rights.
  • Submitting personal data of third parties without a lawful basis.
  • Spam, abuse, and attempts to disrupt the site or the submission process.
  • Confirming in a form something that is not true, such as where the authors live or stay, the submitter's age, the agreement of the authors or the statement on generative models.

The operator may decline or remove a submission that breaks these rules. If a confirmation turns out to be untrue, the operator removes the record under the Takedown Policy as soon as the operator learns of it. From 2026-11-22, the operator may also decline further submissions from the same GitHub account or ORCID iD. The operator relies on the confirmations in good faith and does not check them. A work listed on an untrue confirmation does not mean that Sophaxis is offered where the Submission Policy says it is not. The confirmations stay in the public issue, so that what was confirmed, and when, can be shown. Nothing here asks a submitter for a penalty, or for damages beyond what the law provides.

Continuity

Sophaxis is run by one person, and is planned so that it can stop without loss.

  • A slow decision is not a pause: moderation can take several weeks or a few months. A pause is for when the operator cannot work on Sophaxis at all, for example because of illness. If that lasts for more than 30 days, the top page says that new submissions are paused. Listed records stay online.
  • Unless the operator cannot act, a closure is announced on the top page at least 30 days ahead, and new submissions stop first.
  • If Sophaxis closes, the operator will keep the repository with every record public, so that the records stay readable. Each week, the pages and the PDF copies are also saved to the Internet Archive, where every author of the work consents to it.
  • The code and the records are archived at Zenodo with each release, under a DOI that resolves to the latest version: 10.5281/zenodo.24033371.
  • The operator may hand Sophaxis over to another person, to an institution or to a legal entity. A hand-over is announced on the site first, and these terms stay in force until the new operator changes them in the ordinary way.
  • Anyone may copy the repository and continue the work elsewhere under its licences: CC BY 4.0 for the text and MIT for the code. Each listed work keeps its own licence.
  • The DOIs stay with the repositories that issued them, whatever happens to Sophaxis.

Disputes

A dispute about Sophaxis or a listed work is settled by agreement wherever possible.

  1. Write to the operator first, by email to the address on the operator's ORCID record, or in an issue if the matter can be public. Say what the dispute is about and what would settle it.
  2. Both sides then try, in good faith, to reach an agreement by talking. The operator answers within 7 days and does not leave a dispute without reply.
  3. If talking does not settle it within 60 days, either side may propose mediation, such as civil conciliation (minji chotei) at a Japanese summary court, before going to court.

From 2026-11-25, if a dispute is not settled in these ways, it goes to the Utsunomiya District Court or the Utsunomiya Summary Court, as the court of first instance by agreement, for a person who has agreed to these terms in a form; this agreement is not exclusive. Until then, the court of first instance by agreement is, on the same terms, the district court or the summary court that has jurisdiction over the operator's place of residence. For anyone else, the court that the law sets has jurisdiction. The operator's address is given on request, as the Operator and contact page says. This does not take away a right that the law gives a consumer, or a person outside Japan, to bring or defend a case in another court. Nothing in this section stops anyone from asking a court for urgent relief, or from using the Takedown Policy.

People outside Japan

Sophaxis is run from Japan and can be read from anywhere.

  • The same steps apply. Talks and mediation can be held in writing or online, in English or Japanese, so that no one has to travel to Japan to settle a dispute.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of Japan. Where the law of a person's own country gives them protection that cannot be set aside by agreement, such as the protection of consumers, that protection still applies.
  • A person outside Japan who may sue in the courts of their own country under that country's law keeps that right; the agreement on the court above does not take it away.
  • These terms are written in English, and a Japanese translation is published. Where the translation differs from the English text, the English text applies.
