Preprints in Philosophy and Mathematics

Sophaxis lists preprints in philosophy, mathematics, logic and the philosophy of mathematics. Authors deposit their work in an open repository; Sophaxis moderates the submission and gives it a stable record page.

Acceptance is moderation, not peer review. Work written with language models is accepted when the use is disclosed. Sophaxis charges nothing, to authors or to readers.
