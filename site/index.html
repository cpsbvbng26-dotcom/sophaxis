Not offered in 43 places. Sophaxis is not offered where a law requires, or is reported to require, a service offered to people there to have a representative there, to keep personal data there, to register there, or to meet conditions before personal data leaves the place that Sophaxis cannot meet. From the date the Submission Policy gives for each place (2026-11-22, or 2026-11-26 for 15 of them), Sophaxis does not list work by an author who lives in any of these places, or who stays in one between the submission and the listing, even briefly. This depends only on where an author lives or stays, not on nationality, race, ethnic origin or any other personal characteristic: an author of any nationality who lives elsewhere and does not stay there can submit. The laws include Article 27 of the GDPR and of the UK GDPR, Article 13 of the EU Digital Services Act, Article 53 of China's Personal Information Protection Law, section 37(5) of Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, and Article 18(5) of Russia's Federal Law No. 152-FZ. Sophaxis, run unpaid by one person within free services, has no representative anywhere and keeps no data in any of these places. From 2026-11-26, the reasons also include a duty to give the operator's postal address or telephone number before personal data is collected, a duty to confirm a parent's consent for a minor, and a data protection law that could not be read at its source. The places are the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, China, Thailand, Russia, Türkiye, Egypt, Peru, Albania, Monaco, Guernsey, Botswana, Ecuador, Iraq, Indonesia, Rwanda, Zambia, Ethiopia, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kazakhstan, Tunisia, Uganda, Guinea, Madagascar, Bolivia, Costa Rica and Quebec; and, from 2026-11-26, Argentina, Colombia, Ghana, Korea, Mexico, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Uruguay, Zimbabwe, Kenya, Tanzania, South Africa, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar. See the Submission Policy, which gives the law of each, and the Privacy Policy.
Changes to the policies, some in effect since 2026-11-13 and the rest from 2026-11-21 on. Since its publication on 2026-11-13, ahead of the changes below because going on would act against the terms of the two repositories, Sophaxis no longer copies PDFs by script from Figshare or Research Square, whose terms forbid robots and scripts; the authors of a work there can attach the deposited PDF to the submission issue instead. A person under 13 can no longer submit. A work with more than one author is listed only after every other author confirms their own agreement. Text written in the forms is shared under CC0 1.0, and an abstract given in a form under the licence of the work. The submission form asks two more confirmations: that every author consents to the record being published, offered through OAI-PMH and provided to services in the United States, and agreement to the policies. The form also asks for the extent of use of generative models, at one of four levels: none, editing, assistance or drafting. From 2026-11-22, a work is listed only if no author lives in a place where Sophaxis is not offered, as the Submission Policy lists them, or stays in one between the submission and the listing, even briefly, and no author under 18 lives or stays in India; the form asks the submitter to confirm it. The saves to the Internet Archive become a separate box that may be left empty. From 2026-11-23, a new work is listed only if, on the day its submission issue is opened, at least six months have passed since the first DOI of the work was registered; a new version of a listed work is not counted again. From the same day, a work for which Crossref records a retraction, a withdrawal or a removal is not listed, and one with an expression of concern goes to the operator first. From the same day, a submitter who is not yet an author of a listed work needs a researcher whose ORCID record lists a journal article, a book chapter or a book to vouch for it. From 2026-11-24, the form asks three more statements, on funding, on competing interests, and on human participants and research ethics, and a work is listed only if each is given; it also asks, optionally, where the data and code are. Confirming something untrue in a form is not allowed; from 2026-11-22, the operator may decline further submissions from the account that did so. From 2026-11-25, the court of first instance agreed in a form, for a dispute not settled by talks or mediation, is the Utsunomiya District Court or the Utsunomiya Summary Court, in place of the district court or the summary court that has jurisdiction over the operator's place of residence; the agreement stays non-exclusive, and the rights of consumers and of people outside Japan stay as they are. From 2026-11-26, that agreement on the court covers only suits based on the Terms of Use, or on the submission, listing, correction, withdrawal or removal of the person's own works under them; for any other dispute, the court that the law sets has jurisdiction. From 2026-11-26, Sophaxis is also not offered in 15 more places, and a work is not listed if an author lives in one of them, or stays in one between the submission and the listing: Argentina, Colombia, Ghana, Korea, Mexico, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Uruguay, Zimbabwe, Kenya, Tanzania, South Africa, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar. The reasons are laws read on 2026-11-12 that ask for registration, for conditions before personal data leaves the place, for the operator's postal address or telephone number before personal data is collected, or for a parent's consent that the service confirms before the data of a minor is processed, and data protection laws that could not be read at their source; the Submission Policy gives each. From 2026-11-26, in a dispute the operator aims to answer within 7 days rather than promising to, and during a pause described under "Continuity" in the Terms of Use the answer waits until the pause ends; the call for advisers says the same of its messages. From 2026-11-29, Sophaxis is also not offered in a place where the operator judges that offering Sophaxis would not conform to the law of that place; when the operator so judges, the place and the reason are added to the table of the Submission Policy, and the change to the table is announced on this page before it takes effect. From 2026-12-01, for a submission made on or after that day, the Terms of Use limit the operator's liability for loss caused by negligence that is not gross negligence to the loss that would ordinarily arise, never for intent or gross negligence and never toward a reader who has not agreed to them; and the submitter undertakes that each confirmation in the form is true and, after being told of a third party's claim and heard, compensates the operator for the loss that an untrue one causes, as Articles 415 and 416 of Japan's Civil Code provide. Sophaxis lists preprints in philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science. Authors deposit their work in an open repository; Sophaxis moderates the submission and gives it a stable record page.

Acceptance is moderation, not peer review. The quality of listed work is not guaranteed. Work made with language models or other generative models is accepted when the use is disclosed. Sophaxis charges nothing, to authors or to readers.

Why Sophaxis is run: the Statement of Purpose. It creates no rights or obligations; the policies apply.

Last updated: 2026-11-14. Records are added as they are accepted, and every record is checked once a week.
