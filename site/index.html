Sophaxis is not offered where a law requires, or is reported to require, a service offered to people there to have a representative there, to keep personal data there, to register there, or to meet conditions before personal data leaves the place that Sophaxis cannot meet. From 2026-11-22 Sophaxis does not list work by an author who lives in any of these places, or who stays in one between the submission and the listing, even briefly. This depends only on where an author lives or stays, not on nationality, race, ethnic origin or any other personal characteristic: an author of any nationality who lives elsewhere and does not stay there can submit. The laws include Article 27 of the GDPR and of the UK GDPR, Article 13 of the EU Digital Services Act, Article 53 of China's Personal Information Protection Law, section 37(5) of Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, and Article 18(5) of Russia's Federal Law No. 152-FZ. Sophaxis, run unpaid by one person within free services, has no representative anywhere and keeps no data in any of these places. The places are the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, China, Thailand, Russia, Türkiye, Egypt, Peru, Albania, Monaco, Guernsey, Botswana, Ecuador, Iraq, Indonesia, Rwanda, Zambia, Ethiopia, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kazakhstan, Tunisia, Uganda, Guinea, Madagascar, Bolivia, Costa Rica and Quebec. See the Submission Policy, which gives the law of each, and the Privacy Policy.
Change to the policies from 2026-11-21. A person under 13 can no longer submit. A work with more than one author is listed only after every other author confirms their own agreement. Text written in the forms is shared under CC0 1.0, and an abstract given in a form under the licence of the work. The submission form asks two more confirmations: that every author consents to the record being published, offered through OAI-PMH and provided to services in the United States, and agreement to the policies. The form also asks for the extent of use of generative models, at one of four levels: none, editing, assistance or drafting. From 2026-11-22, a work is listed only if no author lives in a place where Sophaxis is not offered, as the Submission Policy lists them, or stays in one between the submission and the listing, even briefly, and no author under 18 lives or stays in India; the form asks the submitter to confirm it. The saves to the Internet Archive become a separate box that may be left empty. From 2026-11-23, a new work is listed only if, on the day its submission issue is opened, at least six months have passed since the first DOI of the work was registered; a new version of a listed work is not counted again. From the same day, a work for which Crossref records a retraction, a withdrawal or a removal is not listed, and one with an expression of concern goes to the operator first. From the same day, a submitter who is not yet an author of a listed work needs a researcher whose ORCID record lists a journal article, a book chapter or a book to vouch for it. From 2026-11-24, the form asks three more statements, on funding, on competing interests, and on human participants and research ethics, and a work is listed only if each is given; it also asks, optionally, where the data and code are. Confirming something untrue in a form is not allowed; from 2026-11-22, the operator may decline further submissions from the account that did so. From 2026-11-25, the court of first instance agreed in a form, for a dispute not settled by talks or mediation, is the Utsunomiya District Court or the Utsunomiya Summary Court, in place of the district court or the summary court that has jurisdiction over the operator's place of residence; the agreement stays non-exclusive, and the rights of consumers and of people outside Japan stay as they are. See the policies.

Sophaxis lists preprints in philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science. Authors deposit their work in an open repository; Sophaxis moderates the submission and gives it a stable record page.

Acceptance is moderation, not peer review. The quality of listed work is not guaranteed. Work made with language models or other generative models is accepted when the use is disclosed. Sophaxis charges nothing, to authors or to readers.

Last updated: 2026-11-07. Records are added as they are accepted, and every record is checked once a week.
