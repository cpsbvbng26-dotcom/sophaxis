What Sophaxis is

Sophaxis is a free overlay preprint server for philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science. It keeps no manuscripts of its own. Each record links a moderated preprint, deposited with a DOI in an open repository, and keeps an unmodified copy of its PDF where the licence allows. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers.

Pointing readers to it

  • Link the listing or one of the field pages from a subject guide.
  • Each field has its own Atom feed, such as Philosophy, and every new record is in feed.xml.
  • A record can be cited by the DOI of the work; the record page gives the citation in several styles and as BibTeX, RIS and CSL-JSON.

Harvesting the records

  • OAI-PMH: the base URL is https://sophaxis.vercel.app/oai, with the formats oai_dc and oai_openaire.
  • Every record is also in papers.json, and the sitemap lists every page.
  • The record data in feed.xml, papers.json and the OAI-PMH interface may be reused freely. The abstracts and the PDF copies keep the licence of each work, as the Licence Policy says.

Telling researchers

  • Your first preprint is for those who have never made a preprint public.
  • Before you submit gathers what a submission needs.
  • Sophaxis on one page can be printed for a notice board.

What Sophaxis is not

  • It is not a journal and issues no DOIs.
  • It is not run by an institution. One person runs it unpaid, as the Disclaimer says.
  • It has no affiliation with any library, repository or index.

Sophaxis is an overlay preprint server. It charges nothing, to authors or to readers. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review, and the quality of listed work is not guaranteed.
