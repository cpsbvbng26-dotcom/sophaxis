# In plain words

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

Japanese: [やさしいことばで（日本語訳）](./easy.ja.md). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

This page says, in short sentences, what Sophaxis is and how to send a work. The rules are in the [policies](./policy.md). If this page and the rules say different things, the rules count.

## What Sophaxis is

Sophaxis is a website.
It shows papers in philosophy and mathematics.
The papers are kept in other places, called repositories.
Sophaxis shows a page for each paper.
Sophaxis is free.

## What Sophaxis does not do

Sophaxis does not check if a paper is right.
A person only checks if the paper fits the rules.
Sophaxis does not take money.

## How to send a paper

1. Put your paper in a repository, such as Zenodo. You get a DOI.
2. Make an account on ORCID and on GitHub. Join them together.
3. On GitHub, fill in the form and write the DOI.
4. A program checks the form. Then a person decides.
