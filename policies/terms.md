## What Sophaxis provides

Sophaxis lists preprints in philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science.
It gives each listed work a record page, a search, a feed and, where the repository allows, a copy of the PDF.
It does this free of charge.

## What is not allowed

- Submitting work of which you are not an author.
- Submitting material that infringes copyright or other rights.
- Submitting personal data of third parties without a lawful basis.
- Spam, abuse, and attempts to disrupt the site or the submission process.

The operator may decline or remove a submission that breaks these rules.
If a confirmation turns out to be untrue, the operator removes the record under the [Takedown Policy](./takedown.html) as soon as the operator learns of it. From 2026-11-22, the operator may also decline further submissions from the same GitHub account or ORCID iD.
The operator relies on the confirmations in good faith and does not check them. A work listed on an untrue confirmation does not mean that Sophaxis is offered where the [Submission Policy](./submission.html) says it is not.
The confirmations stay in the public issue, so that what was confirmed, and when, can be shown.
Nothing here asks a submitter for a penalty, or for damages beyond what the law provides.

## Continuity

Sophaxis is run by one person, and is planned so that it can stop without loss.

- A slow decision is not a pause: moderation can take several weeks or a few months. A pause is for when the operator cannot work on Sophaxis at all, for example because of illness. If that lasts for more than 30 days, the top page says that new submissions are paused. Listed records stay online.
- Unless the operator cannot act, a closure is announced on the top page at least 30 days ahead, and new submissions stop first.
- If Sophaxis closes, the operator will keep the repository with every record public, so that the records stay readable. Each week, the pages and the PDF copies are also saved to the Internet Archive, where every author of the work consents to it.
- The code and the records are archived at Zenodo with each release, under a DOI that resolves to the latest version: [10.5281/zenodo.24033371](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24033371).
- The operator may hand Sophaxis over to another person, to an institution or to a legal entity. A hand-over is announced on the site first, and these terms stay in force until the new operator changes them in the ordinary way.
- Anyone may copy the repository and continue the work elsewhere under its licences: CC BY 4.0 for the text and MIT for the code. Each listed work keeps its own licence.
- The DOIs stay with the repositories that issued them, whatever happens to Sophaxis.

## Disputes

A dispute about Sophaxis or a listed work is settled by agreement wherever possible.

1. Write to the operator first, by email to the address on the operator's [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-1406-0547), or in an issue if the matter can be public. Say what the dispute is about and what would settle it.
2. Both sides then try, in good faith, to reach an agreement by talking. The operator answers within 7 days and does not leave a dispute without reply.
3. If talking does not settle it within 60 days, either side may propose mediation, such as civil conciliation (minji chotei) at a Japanese summary court, before going to court.

If a dispute is not settled in these ways, it goes to the district court or the summary court that has jurisdiction over the operator's place of residence, as the court of first instance by agreement, for a person who has agreed to these terms in a form; this agreement is not exclusive.
For anyone else, the court that the law sets has jurisdiction.
The operator's address is given on request, as the [Operator and contact](../contact.html) page says.
This does not take away a right that the law gives a consumer, or a person outside Japan, to bring or defend a case in another court.
Nothing in this section stops anyone from asking a court for urgent relief, or from using the [Takedown Policy](./takedown.html).
