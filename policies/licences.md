# Licence Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12.

Japanese: [ライセンスの規程（日本語訳）](./licences.ja.md). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

The Licence Policy names the licences under which a work can be listed, and the licences of this site.

## Accepted licences

Sophaxis lists only work under one of these licences:

- CC BY 4.0
- CC BY-SA 4.0
- CC0 1.0

They let Sophaxis show the abstract and keep a copy of the PDF, with credit to the authors.
They also let readers reuse the work freely.
Licences with NonCommercial or NoDerivatives terms are not accepted.

## Where the licence must appear

The licence must appear in the DOI metadata of the deposit; the automatic check reads it there.
The licence condition applies in every repository.
On arXiv, choose CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0 when you submit; the arXiv non-exclusive licence is not accepted.
Work on SSRN is accepted only if it carries one of the accepted licences.

## Licences of this site

The text of this site is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
Texts by others that this repository quotes, or keeps to verify its quotations, are not licensed under CC BY 4.0 and remain their owners'.
The code is licensed under MIT.
Each listed work keeps its own licence.

The record data in `feed.xml`, `papers.json` and the OAI-PMH interface may be reused freely.
Abstracts in them keep the licence of their work.

## Text written in the forms

From 2026-11-21, an abstract given in a form is shared under the licence of the work.
From 2026-11-21, other text written in the forms, such as the statement on generative models, a reason for withdrawal or a correction, is shared under CC0 1.0 by the person who writes it.
This lets Sophaxis show it with the record, and lets others reuse it with the record data.
Text written in the forms before 2026-11-21 is shown with the record by the permission its writer gave by writing it in a public form for the record; anyone who wrote it can ask for it to be removed under the [Takedown Policy](./takedown.md).
