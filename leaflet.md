Japanese: [一枚でわかる Sophaxis（日本語訳）](./leaflet.ja.md). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

This page is Sophaxis on one page: what it is, what it is not, and how to submit.

This page is meant to fit on one sheet of paper, for printing or for handing on.

## What it is

Sophaxis is a free overlay preprint server for philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science.
It lists preprints that their authors have already deposited in an open repository with a DOI, and gives each a stable record page.

## What it is not

- It does not peer review. Acceptance is moderation.
- It is not a journal, and it issues no DOIs.
- It charges nothing, to authors or to readers.

## How to submit

1. Deposit the work in an open repository under CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0.
2. Link your ORCID record to your GitHub account.
3. Open a submission issue with the DOI. An automatic check answers, and a moderator decides in public.

The [checklist](./checklist.md) gathers what a submission needs, and [From submission to listing](./process.md) shows each step.
