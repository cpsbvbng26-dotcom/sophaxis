# A call to researchers

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

Japanese: [研究者の方へ（日本語訳）](./call.ja.md). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

## Who it is for

Sophaxis is for researchers in philosophy, mathematics, logic, the philosophy of mathematics and the philosophy of science.
It lists a preprint that you have already deposited, with a DOI, in one of the repositories that the [Submission Policy](./policies/submission.md) names.
Every work listed so far is by the operator; the first submissions from other researchers are welcome.

## What a listed work gets

- A record page with the abstract, the versions, the licence, the formats for citing it and an unmodified PDF copy where the repository allows one.
- A place beside other work in its fields, in the lists, the feeds, the search and the collections.
- Its record offered to harvesters through OAI-PMH, and a card that shows the title and the authors when the page is shared.
- A badge that the authors can show, with a link to the record.

Nothing is charged, to authors or to readers.
The authors keep their copyright, and the work stays in its repository under its own DOI.

## What it is not

Acceptance is moderation, not peer review.
Sophaxis does not judge whether a work is correct or important, and listing does not replace publication in a journal.
A listed work can still be published in a journal, and the record page then shows the published version.

## What a submission needs

- A DOI in one of the repositories that the Submission Policy names.
- CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0 in the DOI metadata.
- An ORCID record that links the GitHub account from which you submit.
- A statement on the use of language models and other generative models.
- From 2026-11-23, six months since the first DOI of the work was registered, and, for a first submission, a researcher who vouches for it.
- From 2026-11-24, statements on funding, on competing interests, and on human participants and research ethics.
- No author who lives in, or stays in, a place where Sophaxis is not offered, as the Submission Policy lists them.

## How to submit

The steps are on the page [From submission to listing](./process.md), and the form opens from [Submit](https://sophaxis.vercel.app/submit.html).
A question before submitting can go in [a public issue](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/issues/new), or by email to the address on the operator's [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-1406-0547).
