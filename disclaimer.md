## Listed work

Sophaxis does not check proofs, arguments or data, and does not guarantee that any listed work is correct, original, complete or of any quality.
The authors bear full responsibility for their work, including its statement on generative models, which Sophaxis does not verify.

## Metadata

Titles, authors, abstracts, licences, versions and dates come from the DOI metadata held by DataCite, Crossref and JaLC, and are shown as received; an abstract that the metadata does not have comes from the submission form.
An error there can appear here.
Errors can be reported through the link on each record page.

## The identity check

The automatic check confirms that the person who controls an ORCID record opened the submission issue, and that the ORCID iD belongs to an author of the work.
It is not an official identity check.

This use does not change what is listed or how.

GitHub and Vercel each provide their services "as is", without warranty, and limit their liability under their own terms; Sophaxis cannot promise more about their services than they do.

## Availability

The site is provided as it is, and may be unavailable, change or close.
If it closes, the repository with every record stays public; see Continuity in the [Terms of Use](./policies/terms.html).

Sophaxis depends on outside services:

- If Vercel stops, the site is not served; the records stay in the repository on GitHub and in the Internet Archive.
- If GitHub stops, no one can submit, and the automatic checks do not run; the site keeps showing what it last built.
- If DataCite, Crossref or JaLC cannot be reached, a submission cannot be checked until they can.
- If a repository removes a deposit, its DOI stops resolving, and the record is withdrawn under the [Withdrawal Policy](./policies/withdrawal.html).
