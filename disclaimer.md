## Listed work

Listing is moderation, not peer review.
Sophaxis does not check proofs, arguments or data, and does not guarantee that any listed work is correct, original, complete or of any quality.
The authors bear full responsibility for their work, including its statement on generative models, which Sophaxis does not verify.

## Metadata

Titles, authors, abstracts, licences, versions and dates come from the DOI metadata held by DataCite, Crossref and JaLC, and are shown as received; an abstract that the metadata does not have comes from the submission form.
An error there can appear here.
Errors can be reported through the link on each record page.

## The identity check

The automatic check confirms that the person who controls an ORCID record opened the submission issue, and that the ORCID iD belongs to an author of the work.
It is not an official identity check.

### When a notice comes

1. A notice comes by [the rights holder's form](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/issues/new?template=rights.yml), the same form [in Japanese](https://github.com/cpsbvbng26-dotcom/sophaxis/issues/new?template=rights-ja.yml), or the private route by email to the address on the operator's [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-1406-0547).
2. The operator reads it.
3. While deciding, the operator may add a note where the name appears, saying that Sophaxis is not connected with the rights holder.
4. If the right is confirmed, the operator changes the title and the text of the site, the name of the Vercel project and the address of the site, the name of the repository, the forms and the README.
5. The operator also changes the names of the pages of Sophaxis on other services, as far as each service allows.
6. Some things are not rewritten. The versions archived at Zenodo, past entries of the news, of the notice of changes and of the changelog, and the history of the repository keep the old name. Where the site shows them, it adds a note.
7. The PDF copies of listed works are the authors' files and are not changed.
8. Every change is recorded in the changelog of the public repository, and the decision is posted in the issue or sent by email to a person who wrote by email.
9. If the right is not confirmed, the operator says why in the same way.

The [Takedown Policy](./policies/takedown.md#for-rights-holders), under "For rights holders", sets out the same procedure in brief.

## Availability

The site is provided as it is, and may be unavailable, change or close.
If it closes, the repository with every record stays public; see Continuity in the [Terms of Use](./policies/terms.md).

Sophaxis depends on outside services:

- If Vercel stops, the site is not served; the records stay in the repository on GitHub and in the Internet Archive.
- If GitHub stops, no one can submit, and the automatic checks do not run; the site keeps showing what it last built.
- GitHub's terms let it restrict the use of GitHub Actions, and Actions can stop. While they do not run, no step on the page [What runs by itself](./automation.md) runs by itself. The operator takes those steps by hand, as soon as the operator can; a record page reported for exposed personal data is then taken offline by the operator, not at once.
- If DataCite, Crossref or JaLC cannot be reached, a submission cannot be checked until they can.
- If a repository removes a deposit, its DOI stops resolving, and the record is withdrawn under the [Withdrawal Policy](./policies/withdrawal.md).
