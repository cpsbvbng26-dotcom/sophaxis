# The policies in brief

Last updated: 2026-11-11.

Japanese: [規程の要点（日本語訳）](./rules.ja.md). It is a translation; where the two differ, the English text applies.

This page puts each policy in a few sentences. It is a summary and binds no one; each policy, linked below, is what counts.

## Terms of Use

- The authors bear full responsibility for their works and their listing. Acceptance is moderation, not peer review.
- Sophaxis gives no warranty, and a closure is announced on the top page at least 30 days ahead unless the operator cannot act.
- The laws of Japan apply. A dispute is settled by agreement wherever possible: write to the operator first, and the operator answers within 7 days.
- Read in full: [Terms of Use](./policies/terms.md).

## Privacy Policy

- The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other servers.
- A submission is a public GitHub issue. From an ORCID record, only the name, the websites and the works are used.
- Some services that Sophaxis uses are outside Japan, mostly in the United States; the policy names each.
- Read in full: [Privacy Policy](./policies/privacy.md).

## Submission Policy

- An author submits by the DOI of a work deposited in an accepted repository, under CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 1.0, with an ORCID record that links the submitter's GitHub account.
- Every author must agree. The use of generative models must be disclosed; no model can be an author.
- Sophaxis is not offered to authors who live in, or stay in, certain places, which the policy lists.
- Read in full: [Submission Policy](./policies/submission.md).

## Moderation Policy

- A moderator decides in public, in the issue, and names the reasons for declining.
- The submitter can answer in the issue, and the operator looks once more.
- The operator's own submissions are moderated under the same rules and marked as such.
- Read in full: [Moderation Policy](./policies/moderation.md).

## Licence and Copyright Policies

- Authors keep their copyright. Sophaxis shows the abstract and keeps a copy of the PDF under the work's licence.
- Figures, tables and long quotations from others need permission, or a use the law allows.
- The record data may be reused freely; the site's text is under CC BY 4.0 and its code under MIT.
- Read in full: [Licence Policy](./policies/licences.md) and [Copyright Policy](./policies/copyright.md).

## Withdrawal and Takedown Policies

- An author can withdraw a listed work. The record stays, so that citations do not break, with a notice, and the PDF copy is taken down.
- A record can be removed for legal reasons or at an author's request; what a removed record leaves is set out in the policy.
- Read in full: [Withdrawal Policy](./policies/withdrawal.md) and [Takedown Policy](./policies/takedown.md).
